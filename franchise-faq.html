Common questions

How does the payment work?

Basic Package: ₱140,000 to start, then the remaining ₱140,000 balance and a refundable ₱20,000 Commissary Bond within 45 days of signing.
Grab & Go Drink Bar: ₱200,000 to start, then the remaining ₱200,000 balance and the same refundable ₱20,000 bond within 45 days.
We'll walk you through the complete fee schedule at your tasting, before anything is signed.

Which package should I choose?

If you already run a business and have room for a small counter, the Basic Package costs less to start. If you're opening Sago Loco as its own store in an empty space, choose the Grab & Go Drink Bar. It includes the renovation and setup you'll need.

Do prices change outside Metro Manila?

Yes, both packages may cost a little more or less outside NCR (Metro Manila). We'll confirm your exact price for your location when we talk.

What's included in training?

Both packages include training on how to run a Sago Loco store: making the drinks, setting up your station, and day-to-day operations, before you open.

How soon can I open?

About 45 to 60 days once your location is approved. That covers build-out, equipment, training, and stocking. Finding and securing your space happens before that clock starts, so start looking early.

What happens after I send an inquiry?

We'll contact you within 24 hours to schedule your free product tasting. You'll try the drinks, we'll walk through the packages and the numbers, and you decide after. There's no pressure to commit.
