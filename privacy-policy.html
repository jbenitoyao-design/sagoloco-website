Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BRK Concept Frontiers OPC, operator of the Sago Loco Pearl Shakes brand ("Sago Loco," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information gathered through our website (sagoloco.com), our Facebook Messenger franchise assistant, and related SMS communications (together, our "Channels").

We are committed to protecting your privacy in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its Implementing Rules and Regulations. By using our Channels, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information directly from you when you interact with our Channels:

ChannelInformation collected
Website franchise inquiry formFull name, email address, mobile number, preferred location, and your message
Facebook Messenger franchise assistantYour name and Facebook profile information (as provided by Meta), your mobile number, and the details you share during the conversation
SMS follow-upsYour mobile number, used to send franchise inquiry confirmations and follow-up messages
Website analyticsAnonymous, aggregate traffic data only (see Section 4). We do not collect personal information through analytics.

We do not knowingly collect financial account numbers, government IDs, or other sensitive personal information through our Channels. Please do not send this kind of information to us through the inquiry form, Messenger, or SMS.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your franchise inquiry and answer your questions
  • Send confirmation and follow-up messages about your inquiry, by email, Messenger, or SMS
  • Maintain an internal record of franchise leads for our sales and franchising team
  • Improve our website and franchise onboarding process
  • Comply with legal or regulatory requirements, if applicable

We do not use your information for purposes unrelated to your franchise inquiry without asking you first.

3. How We Store and Share Your Information

Franchise inquiry details are stored in a private, access-controlled Google Sheet used only by the Sago Loco / BRK Concept Frontiers OPC team.

We use the following third-party service providers to operate our Channels; each handles data as your data processor, under our instructions:

  • Google (Google Sheets / Apps Script) — stores and processes website inquiry submissions
  • Meta / Facebook Messenger (via ManyChat) — powers our Messenger franchise assistant; Messenger interactions are also subject to Meta's own Privacy Policy
  • Semaphore — sends SMS confirmations and follow-up messages to your mobile number
  • Cloudflare — provides cookieless, aggregate website traffic analytics (see Section 4)

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes. We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or a valid request from the National Privacy Commission or another government authority.

4. Website Analytics and Cookies

Our website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-first, cookieless analytics service. It measures aggregate page visits and performance and does not use cookies, does not track you across other websites, and does not collect personal information. Because no cookies are used for this purpose, no cookie consent banner is required.

5. Data Retention

We keep franchise inquiry information for as long as reasonably necessary to process your inquiry, follow up with you, and maintain our business records, or until you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first.

6. Your Rights Under the Data Privacy Act

Under Republic Act No. 10173, you have the right to:

  • Be informed that your personal information will be, is being, or was collected and processed
  • Access your personal information that we hold
  • Request correction of inaccurate or outdated information
  • Object to or withdraw consent for the processing of your information
  • Request the erasure or blocking of your personal information, subject to legal grounds for retention
  • Be indemnified for damages from any inaccurate, incomplete, or unlawfully obtained personal information
  • File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (NPC)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 8.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Channels are intended for individuals who are at least 18 years old or otherwise legally able to enter into a franchise discussion. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The "Effective date" at the top of this page will be updated whenever we make changes. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
